Town Trip! — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Who we are: Droidka, the maker of Town Trip! ("the game").

Contact: shaken-caret.0p@icloud.com

What the game itself stores

Your progress (levels, stars, money, tokens, upgrades, paint, settings) is saved on your device (and, if you use Google Play Games, also in your Google account; see below). The game has no accounts and no sign-in. Deleting the game deletes this data.

Game statistics (GameAnalytics)

To learn how far players get, so we know when to add new levels, the phone version sends anonymous game statistics to GameAnalytics: which shifts are started, won or lost (with the stars earned), daily shifts won or lost, how long and how often the game is played, and technical error reports.

With these, the GameAnalytics software sends a random number created for this installation of the game, basic device information (model, operating system, app version, connection type) and your IP address, from which GameAnalytics works out your approximate country. No names, no account, nothing you type.

You can switch this off at any time in the game: MORE → GAME STATS: OFF.

GameAnalytics handles this information under its own policy: https://www.gameanalytics.com/trust/privacy-notice

Adverts (Google AdMob)

The phone versions show adverts through Google AdMob. To show and measure adverts, Google may collect and use:

  • a device advertising identifier (which you can reset or switch off in your phone's settings),
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • your IP address, which shows your approximate location (country or city),
  • which adverts were shown and whether they were tapped.

Google handles this information under its own policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland) the game asks for your choice before showing personalised adverts, and you can change that choice at any time from the game's menu. If an iPhone or iPad version is released, it will ask Apple's tracking permission before any identifier is used for tracking.

Google Play Games (achievements, leaderboards, saved games)

If you use Google Play Games on your phone, the game signs you in to it automatically. Google then stores your achievements, your leaderboard scores (shown with your Play Games name to other players), and a copy of your game progress, so it can follow you to a new phone. The progress copy is kept in a private area of your own Google account that only this game can read; we don't receive it.

Google handles all of this under its own policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can manage or delete your Play Games profile in the Google Play Games app.

Reminders

If you switch reminders on, the game schedules them on your phone itself. Nothing is sent to us or to any server, and you can switch them off in the game's menu or in your phone's settings.

Purchases

The game has no purchases yet. If it offers them in future, purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your card or payment details. We only learn which item was bought so the game can unlock it.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game and you want related advertising data removed, contact us at the address above.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information, we do not use game statistics for advertising, and we do not collect names, phone numbers, contacts, photos or precise location.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
